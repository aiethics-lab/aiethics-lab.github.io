Differential privacy adds noise calibrated to a query's global sensitivity Δf. Laplace draws from Lap(Δf/ε) and gives pure ε-DP. Gaussian draws from N(0, σ²) with σ = Δf·√(2·ln(1.25/δ))/ε and gives the weaker (ε, δ)-DP.

Every release costs budget. Releasing n noisy answers costs n·ε, not ε — and running both mechanisms on the same data costs both. This panel charges you for all of it, which is why the budget moves faster than you might expect.
