school Why This Matters

ProPublica's 2016 investigation found that COMPAS was biased against Black defendants — they were nearly twice as likely to be falsely flagged as high risk compared to White defendants. Explainability tools like SHAP help reveal when race acts as a hidden driver of predictions, even when the model claims to be "fair."
